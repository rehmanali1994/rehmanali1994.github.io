The transmission of ultrasound through the breast is primarily impacted by sound speed and attenuation. Sound speed primarily affects the shape of the transmitted wavefront by advancing and delaying the wave. Attenuation affect the amplitude of the transmitted wave. (b) Ultrasound backscatter reflected by the tissue is the result of spatial changes in the impedance Z=ρc (a product of sound speed c and density ρ). Although density has a small impact on the waveform transmitted through tissue, it plays a much more significant role on the backscattered reflections, so we theorize that modeling the reflected ultrasound signals should enable imaging of the mass density in the breast.
